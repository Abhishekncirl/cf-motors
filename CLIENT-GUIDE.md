# CF Motor Sales — Owner's Guide

A plain-English guide to running your website. You don't need to be technical or
touch any code. Everything below is done from your **admin panel**.

## Signing in

1. Go to **your-website-address/admin** (e.g. `https://www.cfmotorsales.ie/admin`).
2. Enter the **email and password** you were set up with.
3. You'll land on the **Dashboard** — a quick overview of your stock and enquiries.

> Forgot your password, or need another staff login? Ask your web person — logins
> are created for you (there's no public sign-up, which keeps the site secure).

---

## Adding a car

1. In the left menu, click **Vehicles**, then the **Add vehicle** button (top right).
2. **Add photos first:** click **Add photos** and choose pictures from your phone
   or computer. You can select several at once.
   - Photos are shrunk and optimised automatically — you don't need to resize them.
   - **Drag** a photo to change its order.
   - Click the **star** on a photo to make it the **main photo** (the one shown on
     the stock list). The main photo has a cyan border and a "Main" label.
   - Click the **bin** icon to remove a photo.
3. Fill in the **Key details** (make, model, year, price, mileage, etc.).
   - **Weekly price** is optional — leave it blank and no weekly figure is shown.
   - **Import origin** (UK / Japan / Irish) adds the badge buyers see.
4. Fill in the **Specification** and a **Description** (this is the sales text on
   the car's page — write it however you'd describe the car to a customer).
5. Tick **Featured on homepage** if you want this car in the homepage highlights
   (show up to 6 there).
6. Click **Save vehicle**. It's now live on the website.

If a box is filled in wrong, the form tells you in plain English and won't save
until it's fixed.

## Editing or duplicating a car

- **Edit:** Vehicles → click the car's name → change anything → **Save vehicle**.
- **Duplicate:** on the Vehicles list, click **Duplicate** next to a car. This
  makes a copy (great for two similar cars) — open the copy, swap the photos and
  tweak the details, then save.

---

## Marking a car Reserved or Sold

You have two quick ways:

- **On the Vehicles list:** use the **Status** dropdown next to the car and choose
  **available**, **reserved** or **sold**.
- **Inside a car:** use the **Status** box, or the red **Mark as sold (archive)**
  button at the bottom (it asks you to confirm first).

**What happens:**
- **Available** — shown on the public website.
- **Reserved / Sold** — removed from the public website, but **kept in your records**.
  Nothing is deleted. You can set a car back to **available** any time.

To feature/unfeature a car on the homepage, click the **star** on the Vehicles list.

---

## Checking enquiries

Click **Enquiries** in the left menu. Everything customers send lands here in one
place:

- **General** questions, **vehicle** enquiries, **valuation** (sell/trade-in)
  requests, **sourcing** (import to order) requests, and **finance** enquiries.
- **Unread** enquiries have a cyan dot and a highlighted border.
- Click an enquiry to open it — you'll see the customer's **email and phone**
  (tap to call or email), their message, any extra details (like the car reg or
  budget), and any **photos** they attached.
- Opening an enquiry marks it **read**. Use **Mark actioned** once you've dealt
  with it, so you can see what's still outstanding.
- Use the **filter chips** and **Unread only** to focus the list.

> Tip: you can also get an email whenever a new enquiry arrives — ask your web
> person to switch that on (it's a small one-time setup).

---

## Updating your details (phone, hours, socials, homepage banner)

Click **Settings**. Change any of:

- **Contact:** phone number, WhatsApp, email.
- **Address & Eircode.**
- **Facebook and TikTok** links.
- **Homepage hero image:** paste a photo's web address to use it as the big
  homepage banner.
- **Opening hours** for each day.

Click **Save settings** — the website updates **straight away**, no waiting.

---

## A few good habits

- **Good photos sell cars.** Add several, set the best one as the main photo.
- **Keep stock current.** Mark cars sold/reserved promptly so buyers only enquire
  on what's available.
- **Reply fast.** Most buyers come from a phone — a quick WhatsApp or call wins.
- **Sign out** on shared computers (bottom of the left menu).

Stuck on anything? Contact your web person — they can help without affecting your
live stock.
